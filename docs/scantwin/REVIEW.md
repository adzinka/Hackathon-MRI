# Review: ScanTwin concept and failure catalogue

Reviewed: `ScanTwin a digital twin of the MRI scanner.pdf` (4 pages) and `ScanTwin failure catalogue and QC thresholds.xlsx`
(60 failure modes, decision rules, scan types, context factors, sources), prepared by the team bioengineer, 20 Nov 2026.
Checked against the hackathon dossier, the StarterPack and our physics simulator. All simulation results are SIMULATED.

## Verdict

**Very good fit. The concept matches the architecture we already built almost one to one, and the catalogue
fills the biggest gap we had: a sourced, ID-numbered list of failure modes with metrics, limits and fixes.** It is now
part of the system (see "What we adopted"). Five points need a team decision, listed under "Issues".

| Aspect | Fit | Comment |

## What we adopted

- `catalogue/scantwin_catalogue.json`: the whole workbook, regenerated by `scripts/import_catalogue.py`.
- `twin_evidence/gates.py`: the numeric "Built" MRS rows as gates with their IDs: F08, F12, F13, F14, F15, F20, F21, F23,
  F35 (+ F19, F22 as standing notes). Verdict and deciding gate follow the "Decision rules" sheet; fixes come from the
  catalogue text. Metrics that cannot be computed are shown as N/A with a reason (rule 3), never as FAIL.
- Packet-level rows were already implemented in `validate.py`: F11 system QC, F32 registration, F33 stated vs implied
  uncertainty, F35 protocol, F54 schema, F55 valid range, F60 change test.
- App tab ⑦: ScanTwin-style panel (gates with IDs, deciding gate, "what this result can tell you", fix for the next scan),
  the full catalogue browser, and the threshold check below. The word "diagnosis" is gone from that tab.

## Threshold check with the simulator (`scenario_data/threshold_check.csv`, `sweeps.csv`)

Method from the PDF: add one fault at increasing strength, measure like real data, record how far Cho/Cr moves from the
known truth. "1 SD" = scatter of a clean scan (≈ 0.021 in Cho/Cr). 8 scans per strength.

| Gate | Catalogue WARN | Cho/Cr shift reaches 1 SD at | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| F12 linewidth | > 10 Hz | ≈ 14 Hz | ✅ consistent (slightly conservative) |
| F08 frequency drift | > 6 Hz | ≈ 10 Hz | ✅ consistent (conservative) |
| F20 voxel shift | > 2 mm | ≈ 1.9 mm | ✅ consistent |
| F13 SNR | < 25 | never (bias < 1 SD down to SNR 10) | ✅ protects precision, not bias |
| F23 motion outliers | > 5 % | never | ✅ protects precision, not bias |
| **F15 residual water / NAA** | **> 5** | **≈ 0.3** | ⚠️ **too lenient by ~15×**: at a ratio of 1.4 Cho/Cr is already 4.6 SD low and still PASSes |

## Issues for a team decision (as raised, v1.0)

1. **F15 is too lenient for our pipeline.** Our fit has no residual-water model, so the water tail biases Cr and Cho.
   Options: (a) set F15 WARN > 0.5, FAIL > 1.5 for this pipeline, or (b) add water removal (HLSVD, as LCModel / Osprey
   do) and re-run `scripts/sweep_faults.py`. The limit belongs to the *pipeline*, not only to the scanner; the catalogue
   should say so. Until decided, the app uses the catalogue value and a test (`test_known_gap_water…`) documents the gap.
2. **F19 and F22 would make every StarterPack MRS scan WARN, the baseline included.** PRESS at 3 T always gives ~12 %
   displacement (F19 WARN > 10 %), and the StarterPack has ratios only (F22). Applied as gates, the baseline enters the
   twin with doubled variance and the reference numbers of Gate 1 change. We show both as standing notes. Suggestion:
   F19 → compare the displacement *between* sessions; F22 → a limitation line on the measurement card.
3. **F08 and F23 need the individual transients**, which the StarterPack does not store. They are N/A on real data.
   Recommendation for the data contract: keep transients (NIfTI-MRS dimension DIM_DYN). This is also the only way to tell
   drift (fixed by alignment: 14.8 → 8.9 Hz) from a bad shim (not fixed).
4. **A fault class is missing for MRS: RF transmit (B1) miscalibration.** In the simulator it lowers SNR and leaks water,
   biasing Cho/Cr by −0.12 while F14 (CRLB 11 %) stays PASS. F47 covers B1 only for quantitative maps. Suggest a row
   "¹H MRS · transmitter / flip-angle calibration" (metric: reference-signal amplitude vs phantom baseline).
5. **F14 (CRLB %) alone does not catch bias.** It stays < 20 % in every simulated fault, including the ones that move
   Cho/Cr by 5 SD. That is the dossier's point (CRLB is a lower bound). Keep F14, but never as the only fit check.

Smaller notes: the context factors are good but not yet in the UI (a "scan context" input on tab ⑦ would use them);
F21 needs a per-session tissue segmentation (the StarterPack has one T1 map); F57 (field and frequency both present)
and F59 (de-identification) are easy additions to `validate.py`; the PDF calls the product "ScanTwin", the showcase
page says "Twin Evidence": pick one name for the pitch.

## What the catalogue changed in the results

- **Voxel misplacement is no longer invisible.** With F20 (prescribed voxel centre vs baseline) a 4 mm shift FAILs,
  although the spectrum is perfect. Before, only the tissue fractions could reveal it.
- **Water and B1 faults now PASS** (F15 limit), see issue 1.
- The real November scan: F12 + F13 → WARN, as in the organisers' packet; F08, F21, F23 N/A.
