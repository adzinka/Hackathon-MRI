Issues for a team decision

1.F15 is too lenient for our pipeline. Our fit has no residual-water model, so the water tail biases Cr and Cho. Options: (a) set F15 WARN > 0.5, FAIL > 1.5 for this pipeline, or (b) add water removal (HLSVD, as LCModel / Osprey do) and re-run scripts/sweep_faults.py. The limit belongs to the pipeline, not only to the scanner; the catalogue should say so. Until decided, the app uses the catalogue value and a test (test_known_gap_water…) documents the gap.
2.F19 and F22 would make every StarterPack MRS scan WARN, the baseline included. PRESS at 3 T always gives ~12 % displacement (F19 WARN > 10 %), and the StarterPack has ratios only (F22). Applied as gates, the baseline enters the twin with doubled variance and the reference numbers of Gate 1 change. We show both as standing notes. Suggestion: F19 → compare the displacement between sessions; F22 → a limitation line on the measurement card.
3.F08 and F23 need the individual transients, which the StarterPack does not store. They are N/A on real data. Recommendation for the data contract: keep transients (NIfTI-MRS dimension DIM_DYN). This is also the only way to tell drift (fixed by alignment: 14.8 → 8.9 Hz) from a bad shim (not fixed).
4.A fault class is missing for MRS: RF transmit (B1) miscalibration. In the simulator it lowers SNR and leaks water, biasing Cho/Cr by −0.12 while F14 (CRLB 11 %) stays PASS. F47 covers B1 only for quantitative maps. Suggest a row "¹H MRS · transmitter / flip-angle calibration" (metric: reference-signal amplitude vs phantom baseline).
5.F14 (CRLB %) alone does not catch bias. It stays < 20 % in every simulated fault, including the ones that move Cho/Cr by 5 SD. That is the dossier's point (CRLB is a lower bound). Keep F14, but never as the only fit check.

Smaller notes: the context factors are good but not yet in the UI (a "scan context" input on tab ⑦ would use them);
F21 needs a per-session tissue segmentation (the StarterPack has one T1 map); F57 (field and frequency both present)
and F59 (de-identification) are easy additions to validate.py; the PDF calls the product "ScanTwin", the showcase
page says "Twin Evidence": pick one name for the pitch.

What the catalogue changed in the results

•Voxel misplacement is no longer invisible. With F20 (prescribed voxel centre vs baseline) a 4 mm shift FAILs, although the spectrum is perfect. Before, only the tissue fractions could reveal it.
•Water and B1 faults now PASS (F15 limit), see issue 1.
•The real November scan: F12 + F13 → WARN, as in the organisers' packet; F08, F21, F23 N/A.
