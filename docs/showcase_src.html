The problem

One number went up 23 %. Should anyone act on it?

A brain chemistry ratio (choline to creatine) was 0.69 in May and 0.85 in November. Raw data shout. Our twin reads the evidence first.

How it works

Four steps, every time a scan arrives

1 · Measure

The scanner sends evidence

Every number arrives as a packet with its unit, its uncertainty and the file it came from.

2 · Check

A quality gate decides

Pass, warn or fail. Signal quality, positioning, file integrity, and the scanner’s own health on that day. The gate never trusts a label it can re-check.

3 · Update

The twin moves, by the right amount

Good evidence moves the belief a lot, weak evidence a little, broken evidence not at all.

4 · Advise

It says what to scan next

The scan that removes the most doubt per scanner minute. It advises; it never diagnoses.

Why people can trust it

Built to be questioned

Every number has a source

Each update traces back to a file and its SHA-256 fingerprint. Click any result and see where it came from.

Uncertainty is never hidden

Every value carries its ± . “Not enough evidence yet” is an honest, valid answer.

It re-checks the physics

The twin recomputes signal-to-noise from the raw spectrum instead of trusting the label.

It advises, never diagnoses

Output is the next best measurement, not a medical decision. Clinical use stays with clinicians.

What exists today

Working code, tested against the reference numbers

Built in 24 hours at the InnovateU hackathon in Ulm. A quality gate, a gated Kalman twin, a scanner twin and an interactive app, all reproducing the challenge’s worked example to three decimals, with 17 automated tests.

Now

Brain + scanner twin prototype. Synthetic StarterPack data, open formats (DICOM, NIfTI, BIDS).

Next

Real repeat-scan data. Replace teaching assumptions with measured repeatability.

Then

Real phantom records. Connect a site’s weekly QC to the scanner twin.

Later

Federation. Twins of people, devices and machines that exchange evidence, not raw data.
